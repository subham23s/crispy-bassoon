# Proposed contribution and novelty boundary

CyberSecRA-X proposes an evidence-aware orchestration pattern that exposes provenance, claim support, contradictions, and a project-defined reliability score in one cybersecurity research workflow. The implementation contribution is the integration of deterministic query understanding, hybrid retrieval, source ranking, verification, abstention, paper comparison, and RAG-security controls.

The project does **not** claim first-ever status, scientific validation of CERS, or superior model accuracy. Such claims require a systematic review, baselines, annotated data, statistical tests, and reproducible external experiments.
